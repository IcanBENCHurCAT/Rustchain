# Extraterrestrial Mythos Project

## Overview
This project aims to create a mythos based on extraterrestrial conspiracy theories, incorporating various anomalies and mapping them to archetypes. The mythos will explain how certain traits and characteristics can be attributed to extraterrestrial influences or ancient deities.

## Anomalies
- Green/gray eyes
- Pale skin
- Curly hair
- Extremely high intelligence
- Extreme intuition
- Ability to persuade easily
- Some ability to perform 'manifestation'
- Highly susceptible to others' energy
- Drained over time
- Prone to addictions and confusion
- Delusions

## Archetypes and Influences
- Star Seeds from the Pleiades
- Hybrids
- Ancient Civilizations
- Ancient Deities
- Human Anomalies

## Sources
- Online research
- Wikis
- Groups

## Files
- `research.md`: Compilation of research findings and sources
- `mythos.md`: Drafted mythos based on research
- `anomalies.md`: Detailed descriptions of the anomalies and their mappings
