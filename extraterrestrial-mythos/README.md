# Extraterrestrial Mythos Project

A comprehensive mythos framework based on extraterrestrial conspiracy theories, mapping physical and psychological anomalies to archetypes, and weaving them into a coherent narrative about the origins and roles of human traits attributed to extraterrestrial influences or ancient deities.

```

├── TIMELINE.md            # Chronological arc from First Descent to present

```

## The Five Archetypes

| Archetype | Role | Marker | Power Type |
|-----------|------|--------|------------|
| **Sky Fathers** (Ancient Deities) | Architects, rulers | Pale skin, green/gray eyes, curly hair | Elemental, longevity, advanced tech |
| **Chosen** (Ancient Civilizations) | Builders, teachers | Green/gray eyes, pale skin, curly hair | Knowledge, building, cultural creation |
| **Hybrids** (The Bloodline) | Balancers, operators | Pale skin, green/gray eyes, curly hair, larger eyes | Manifestation, durability, programmed intelligence |
| **Star Seeds** (Pleiadian Children) | Healers, light-bearers | Green/gray eyes, pale skin, curly hair | Authentic intelligence, intuition, charisma, healing |
| **Anomalies** (The Broken) | Seekers, canaries | Sporadic markers | Sensitivity, intuition, disease resistance |
