## Quick Reference

### The Five Archetypes

| Archetype | Role | Physical Markers | Primary Gift | Primary Curse |
|-----------|------|------------------|--------------|---------------|
| **Sky Fathers** (Ancient Deities) | Architects, rulers | Pale skin, green/gray eyes, curly hair, towering | Elemental control, longevity, advanced tech | Biological incompatibility with Earth; internal schism |
| **Chosen** (Ancient Civilizations) | Builders, teachers | Green/gray eyes, pale skin, curly hair | Exceptional intellect, advanced knowledge | Mental tethering to Sky Father will |
| **Hybrids** (The Bloodline) | Balancers, operators | Pale skin, green/gray eyes, curly hair, larger eyes, lower body temp | Manifestation, durability, speed, programmed intelligence | Energy vulnerability, addiction, loneliness, predictability |
| **Star Seeds** (Pleiadian Children) | Healers, light-bearers | Green/gray eyes, pale skin, curly hair | Authentic intelligence, extreme intuition, charisma, healing | Unaware of nature, emotional sensitivity, belonging void |
| **Anomalies** (The Broken) | Seekers, canaries | Sporadic markers (green/gray eyes, pale skin, curly hair) | Heightened sensitivity, disease resistance | Draining, confusion, addiction, delusion, no direction |

### Key Factions
