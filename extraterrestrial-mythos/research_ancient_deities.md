# Research: Ancient Deities

## Anomalies
- **Green/gray eyes**: Common in depictions of deities, symbolizing their extraterrestrial origins.
- **Pale skin**: Represented in ancient art, indicating their divine and otherworldly nature.
- **Curly hair**: Found in ancient art, symbolizing a blend of celestial and terrestrial elements.

## Characteristics
- **Immortality**: Believed to be immortal, existing beyond the bounds of human life.
- **Power over elements**: Ability to control natural elements and phenomena.
- **Influence on human fate**: Significant impact on human destiny and the course of history.
- **Advanced knowledge and technology**: Often depicted with advanced knowledge and technology, suggesting extraterrestrial influence.
- **Depictions in ancient texts and artifacts**: Ancient myths and legends often describe deities with characteristics that resemble extraterrestrial beings.

## Role
- **Protectors and destroyers**: Depending on their nature, they can be protectors or destroyers, guiding or challenging humanity.
