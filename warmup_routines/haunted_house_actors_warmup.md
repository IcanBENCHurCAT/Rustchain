# Haunted House Actor Warmup Routine

## Objective
To prepare the body for physical activity and enhance flexibility, focusing on leg/hips, back, shoulders, and neck.

## Duration
10-15 minutes

## Equipment Needed
None

## Warmup Routine

1. **March on the Spot (3 minutes)**
   - **Description**: Start off marching on the spot and then march forwards and backwards. Pump your arms up and down in rhythm with your steps, keeping the elbows bent and the fists soft.
   - **Benefits**: Increases heart rate, warms up the muscles, and improves coordination.

2. **Knee Lifts (1 minute)**
   - **Description**: Stand tall and bring up alternate knees to touch the opposite hand.
   - **Benefits**: Warms up the legs and improves core stability.

3. **Shoulder Rolls (1 minute)**
   - **Description**: Continue marching on the spot and roll your shoulders forward and backward.
   - **Benefits**: Loosens the shoulder muscles and improves shoulder mobility.

4. **Cat/Cow (2 minutes)**
   - **Description**: Get on your hands and knees. Arch your back and look up (Cow Pose), then round your back and tuck your chin to your chest (Cat Pose). Repeat the sequence slowly.
   - **Benefits**: Stretches the spine and improves spinal mobility.

5. **Lumbar Rotation Stretch (1 minute)**
   - **Description**: Lie on your back and bring both knees to your chest. Slowly rotate your knees to one side while keeping your shoulders on the ground. Hold for 15-30 seconds, then switch sides.
   - **Benefits**: Stretches the lower back and improves lumbar flexibility.

6. **Thoracic Rotation Stretch (1 minute)**
   - **Description**: Sit on the floor with your legs crossed. Place your hands behind your head and twist your torso to one side. Hold for 15-30 seconds, then switch sides.
   - **Benefits**: Stretches the upper back and improves thoracic mobility.

7. **Upper Trap Stretch (1 minute)**
   - **Description**: Stand or sit and tilt your head to one side, bringing your ear towards your shoulder. Use your hand to gently pull your head further to the side. Hold for 15-30 seconds, then switch sides.
   - **Benefits**: Relieves tension in the upper trapezius muscles and improves neck flexibility.

8. **Hip Flexor Stretch (1 minute)**
   - **Description**: Kneel on one knee with the other foot flat on the ground in front of you. Push your hips forward and hold the stretch. Hold for 15-30 seconds, then switch sides.
   - **Benefits**: Stretches the hip flexors and improves hip flexibility.

9. **Hamstring Stretch (1 minute)**
   - **Description**: Sit on the ground with one leg extended and the other bent. Reach forward and try to touch your toes. Hold for 15-30 seconds, then switch sides.
   - **Benefits**: Stretches the hamstrings and improves leg flexibility.

10. **Chest Opener Stretch (1 minute)**
    - **Description**: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and interlock your fingers behind your back. Lift your arms and lean back slightly. Hold for 15-30 seconds.
    - **Benefits**: Opens the chest and improves posture.

11. **Overhead and W Stretch (1 minute)**
    - **Description**: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Extend your arms overhead and then bring them down to form a W shape. Hold for 15-30 seconds.
    - **Benefits**: Stretches the shoulders and upper back.
