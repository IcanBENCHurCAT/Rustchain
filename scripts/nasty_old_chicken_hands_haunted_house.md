# Nasty Old Chicken Hands - Haunted House Script

## Scene Description

The scene is set in a dimly lit, eerie hallway of a haunted house. The walls are adorned with cobwebs, and the air is thick with the smell of mold. A flickering light casts shadows on the walls, making everything seem more sinister. Suddenly, a figure emerges from the darkness, revealing himself to be Nasty Old Chicken Hands.

## Character Description

- **Height:** 6'2"
- **Appearance:** Wears a tattered, blood-stained lab coat. His hands are grotesquely deformed, resembling the talons of a chicken. His eyes are hollow and sunken, giving him a menacing look.
- **Props:** 
  - Rusty scalpel
  - Broken mirror
  - Ancient, dusty tome

## Lines

1. **Nasty Old Chicken Hands:** *creaking voice* Welcome, dear visitor, to the chamber of the forgotten. I am Nasty Old Chicken Hands, and I have been waiting for you...
2. **Nasty Old Chicken Hands:** *leans in, whispering* Do you hear the whispers? They speak of the dark experiments that led to my cursed form...
3. **Nasty Old Chicken Hands:** *raises the rusty scalpel* This is the tool of my trade. With it, I once sought to unlock the secrets of life and death. Now, it is a reminder of my failure...
4. **Nasty Old Chicken Hands:** *points to the broken mirror* Look into the mirror, and you will see the truth. I was once a man, but now I am a monster...
5. **Nasty Old Chicken Hands:** *flips open the ancient tome* Here lies the knowledge that brought me to this fate. But perhaps, it can also set me free...

## Props Usage

- **Rusty Scalpel:** Used to create a sense of unease and danger. Can be used to scratch the walls or cut through the air.
- **Broken Mirror:** Reflects distorted images, enhancing the eerie atmosphere. Can be shattered to reveal hidden messages or symbols.
- **Ancient Tome:** Contains cryptic symbols and incantations. Can be used to recite spells or reveal secrets.

## Additional Details

- **Background Music:** Low, ominous organ music with occasional creaking sounds.
- **Lighting:** Flickering, dim lights that cast long shadows.
- **Special Effects:** Occasional gusts of wind, mist, and ghostly apparitions.
- **Interaction:** Visitors can choose to interact with the props or try to solve the puzzles presented by Nasty Old Chicken Hands.

## Conclusion

Nasty Old Chicken Hands fades back into the shadows, leaving the visitors to ponder the dark secrets they have uncovered. The experience is both terrifying and thought-provoking, leaving a lasting impression on all who dare to enter his chamber.
