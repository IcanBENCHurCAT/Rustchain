## IDEA 1: SeasonStaff (#1)

### Criteria Scores

| # | Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|---|-----------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Market Size | 5 | ~50K entertainment venues in GA, ~200K nationally. Not small, but bounded. |
| 2 | Willingness to Pay | 6 | Venues already pay for Sling/Deputy ($30-60/mo). $49-99/mo is a lift but justifiable. |
| 3 | GTM Difficulty | 6 | Reachable through industry connections, but fragmented. No unified trade association. |
| 4 | Churn Risk | 7 | **High churn.** Seasonal = by definition, you vanish for 3-4 months every year. Venues expect to pause, pausing erodes stickiness. |
| 5 | Competitive Moat | 3 | Very low. Generic tools (Sling, Deputy) could add entertainment features in a sprint. |
| 6 | Revenue Scalability | 5 | At $70/mo avg, 2,000 venues = $140K MRR. Lifestyle business at best ($2-3M ARR). |
| 7 | Multiple Revenue Streams | 6 | $49-99/mo + 2% wage fee. Could add background checks, training modules. |

**Overall Business Score: 5.6/10**

**TAM:** ~200K entertainment venues nationally. 15% adoption = 30K venues. At $70/mo = $2.52M MRR top-of-TAM. Realistic: $500-800K ARR.

**Verdict:** Solid niche business with clear beachhead, but seasonal churn and weak moat make it lifestyle, not venture-scale.

## IDEA 5: LocalLoop (#5)

### Criteria Scores

| # | Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|---|-----------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Market Size | 6 | Expandable from Conyers to other metro areas. 400+ mid-size suburbs × 50 businesses each = 20K businesses. |

| 5 | Competitive Moat | 2 | Very low. Google Maps, Yelp, Facebook Business Pages dominate. Hard to differentiate. |
| 6 | Revenue Scalability | 4 | $29/mo × 5K businesses = $145K MRR top-of-TAM. Lifestyle business. |
| 7 | Multiple Revenue Streams | 4 | Premium listings, event promotion, service booking take rate. All are extensions of directory. |

**Overall Business Score: 4.5/10**

**TAM:** Expandable but each local market is small. Best case: $2-3M ARR if you scale to 50+ cities.

**Verdict:** Hard to monetize, hard to differentiate, hard to bootstrapt network effects. Best as a community project, not a business.
