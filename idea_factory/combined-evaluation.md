## KEY INSIGHTS FROM EACH PERSPECTIVE

### Engineer's Top Takeaways:
- **ShelfSight** needs an ML specialist — not a 2-person team play. The CV model training on messy real-world shelves is the hard part. Once trained, the rest is standard web app.
- **PulseCheck** is the perfect beachhead: simple CRUD, role-based access, stable requirements, guaranteed first customer (Netherworld).
- **BidSnap** has a great risk-mitigation strategy: human-in-the-loop review before proposals are sent means bad AI output doesn't reach customers.

### Scrum Master's Top Takeaways:
- **Build PulseCheck first (6-8 wks), then BidSnap (8-10 wks)** as revenue engines. Use that cash flow to fund ShelfSight R&D.
- **ShelfSight's CV accuracy risk is existential** — if the model never reaches ~90% precision, the product doesn't exist. This isn't a bug you patch, it's a fundamental technical risk.
- **EscapeFlow and LocalLoop** are tied at 6.7 but for different reasons: one is a technical scope creep trap, the other is an operational GTM risk.

### Manager's Top Takeaways:
- **ShelfSight has the strongest business case** — massive TAM, clear ROI for customers, defensible moat, real whitespace. Enterprise competitors charge $50K+. You charge $100.
- **SeasonStaff's seasonal churn is the killer** — if venues only use you Aug-Oct, you're fighting churn every year. No amount of product improvement fixes that business model.
- **BidSnap's pricing is flexible** — pay-per-use ($10/proposal) OR subscription ($49/mo + $5/proposal). Start with pay-per-use to validate, then push subscription.

## WINNER DETERMINATION

### 🏆 **BidSnap** — Best Overall Choice for Starting

**Why BidSnap beats ShelfSight:**
1. **Lower technical risk** — AI image analysis (OpenAI Vision/Claude) is a solved problem. ShelfSight needs custom CV model training.
2. **Faster to revenue** — 8-10 weeks vs 16-20+ weeks. Get paid sooner.
3. **Same TAM category** — contractors and retailers are both massive, underserved markets.
4. **Easier to sell** — contractors literally just need proposals. Retailers need convincing that CV works on messy shelves.
5. **Builds credibility** — shipping a working product is worth more than planning a moonshot.

**Why it's better than PulseCheck:**
1. **Higher revenue potential** — $49/mo + $5/proposal scales better than $59/mo per venue.
2. **Broader market** — all contractors vs. seasonal attractions only.
3. **Same build complexity** — both are 4-8 weeks.

### 🥈 Runner-Up: ShelfSight AI
The highest-upside play if you have access to an ML engineer and can tolerate 16+ weeks before first revenue. Build this AFTER BidSnap generates cash and credibility.

### 🥉 Beachhead Play: PulseCheck
Quickest to ship (6-8 weeks), guaranteed first customer (Netherworld), builds domain knowledge. Good for generating early revenue while working on BidSnap.
