## 🏆 WINNER: BidSnap

**"Turn a phone photo into a winning proposal in 60 seconds."**

### Why BidSnap
- **Market:** 3.8M contractors in US, all losing bids because proposals take too long
- **Technical:** 6-8 week MVP, off-the-shelf AI (Claude), proven stack
- **Delivery:** Clean scope, one stakeholder type, human-in-the-loop risk containment
- **Business:** $49/mo subscription, $150K+ ARR path at 250 customers, whitespace vs bloated competitors
- **Garret Fit:** Understands contractor workflows, can reach pilot contractors through local networks

### Runner-Ups
1. **ShelfSight AI** — Highest upside (2.7M retailers, $12M+ ARR path) but needs ML specialist, 16-20 week MVP
2. **PulseCheck** — Quickest to ship (4-6 weeks), guaranteed first customer (Netherworld), good for early revenue
