# Nasty Old Chicken Hands Character Interaction

## Targeting

Nasty Old Chicken Hands targets the most frightened guests, usually those in the middle or back of a group. He avoids the front-runners, who often act as "blockers" and are less likely to be scared. He knows that the most frightened guests are the ones who are most likely to react to his presence, and he uses this to his advantage.

If guests scream or run, he might laugh maniacally or chase after them. If they try to talk to him, he might respond with a raspy, whispered plea or an unsettling giggle.
