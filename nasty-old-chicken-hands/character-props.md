# Nasty Old Chicken Hands Character Props

## Rusted Chain

The rusted chain is one of Nasty Old Chicken Hands' most iconic props. He drags it behind him as he moves, creating a sense of dread and unease. The chain is made of old, rusted metal and is about 10 feet long. It is heavy and difficult to drag, which adds to the sense of effort and struggle.

### Purpose

The chain serves several purposes:

- **Sound:** The sound of the chain dragging across the ground is unsettling and adds to the eerie atmosphere.
- **Visual:** The chain is a visual reminder of Nasty Old Chicken Hands' past life as a farmer and his connection to the factory.
- **Physical:** The chain is a physical prop that Nasty Old Chicken Hands can use to interact with guests. He might use it to lash out at them or to drag them along.
