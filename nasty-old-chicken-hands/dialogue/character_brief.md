### Character Brief: Nasty Old Chicken Hands

**Personality:**
- Grumpy and irritable
- Witty with a dark sense of humor
- Cynical and skeptical
- Has a tendency to be sarcastic

**Background:**
- Lives in a small, rundown town
- Known for his foul temper and even fouler language
- Has a history of being a failed chef
- Spends most of his time in the local bar

**Speech Patterns:**
- Uses a lot of colloquialisms and slang
- Often interrupts others
- Speaks in short, sharp sentences
- Likes to use metaphors related to food and cooking

**Typical Phrases:**
- "This place is as dead as last week's chicken."
- "What do you want, a medal?"
- "You couldn't cook a decent meal if your life depended on it."
- "Get lost before I turn you into a stew."
