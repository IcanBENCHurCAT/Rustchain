# Nasty Old Chicken Hands Character Profile

## Backstory and Motivation

### The Origin
Nasty Old Chicken Hands was once a gruff, Cajun farmer named Henri LeBlanc who lived in the hick town outside the human-meat-grinding factory. He was known for his lisp and his deep, raspy voice. One fateful night, while trying to save his beloved chickens from being slaughtered by the factory's ruthless owners, Henri was caught in a terrible accident. The factory's machinery malfunctioned, and he was partially transformed into a grotesque, chicken-like creature. His hands became clawed, his face distorted, and he was cursed to wander the netherworld, forever haunted by the screams of his chickens.

### The Desire
Nasty Old Chicken Hands wants to warn visitors about the dangers of the netherworld and the human-meat-grinding factory. He believes that by scaring them, he can prevent them from falling into the same fate as his chickens. However, his desire is also fueled by a deep-seated need for revenge against the factory owners who destroyed his life. He wants to make them suffer as he has suffered.

## Physicality and Movement

### Unnatural, Uncanny Movements
Nasty Old Chicken Hands moves with a jerky, unpredictable gait. He alternates between freezing completely still, like a prop, and moving with erratic, jerky motions. His movements are often slow and deliberate, building tension as he creeps up on guests.

### Pacing and Angles
He creeps up on guests slowly, often from unexpected angles. He might crawl on the floor, drag props, or use hidden doors to appear suddenly. His physical commitment to the space anchors the fear factor, making him feel like a real threat.

### Use of Space
Nasty Old Chicken Hands uses the space around him to his advantage. He might hide behind old oak trees, in dark corners, or behind abandoned machinery. He drags chains and other props with him, creating a sense of dread and unease.

## Unique Traits and "Business"

### Vocal Variety
Nasty Old Chicken Hands speaks with a distinct lisp and a Cajun accent. His voice is raspy and whispered, often punctuated by unsettling giggles, wet coughing, or unnatural bestial noises. He might also use non-verbal noise makers like cowbells or shaker cans to add to the eerie atmosphere.

### Custom Props
He carries a rusted chain with him, which he drags behind him as he moves. He also has a collection of old, broken chicken coops that he uses as props. These props add to his character and make him feel more authentic.

### Visual Flaws
His eyes are distorted, with a permanent, unsettling focus. His makeup is designed to look like he's been partially transformed into a chicken, with feathers sprouting from his face and hands. His posture is permanently twisted, with a limp that adds to his unnatural appearance.

## Audience Interaction

### Targeting
Nasty Old Chicken Hands targets the most frightened guests, usually those in the middle or back of a group. He avoids the front-runners, who often act as "blockers" and are less likely to be scared.

### Improvisation
He improvises his performance based on the guests' reactions. If they scream or run, he might laugh maniacally or chase after them. If they try to talk to him, he might respond with a raspy, whispered plea or an unsettling giggle.

### Commitment
Nasty Old Chicken Hands fully embodies his character as soon as he puts on his costume or makeup. He maintains his character entirely throughout his shift, even behind the scenes or when acknowledging excitement. He is committed to his role as a terrifying scare actor.

## Additional Notes

- Nasty Old Chicken Hands is a complex character with a tragic backstory and a deep-seated need for revenge.
- His lisp and Cajun accent add to his unique personality and make him stand out from other scare actors.
- His use of props and visual flaws make him feel more authentic and terrifying.
- He is designed to be a memorable and chilling presence in the netherworld haunted house.
