### Nasty Old Chicken Hands Backstory

#### Origin
- **Birthplace**: A small, forgotten town near Stone Mountain, Georgia.
- **Family**: Raised by a single mother who worked as a fortune teller at local carnivals and fairs.
- **Early Life**: Lived in poverty, often helping his mother with her readings and performances. Developed a deep fascination with the supernatural and the art of deception.

#### Motivations
- **Desire for Power**: Driven by a relentless desire to gain control over his fate and the lives of others.
- **Fear of Abandonment**: Haunted by the memory of his father's abandonment, he seeks to create a family of his own, even if it means binding others to him through dark magic.
- **Need for Recognition**: Craves recognition and respect, often feeling overlooked and undervalued in his early years.

#### Key Life Events
- **First Encounter with the Occult**: At the age of 12, he discovered an ancient book of spells hidden in his mother's trunk. This book would become his most prized possession and guide him throughout his life.
- **Joining Netherworld Haunted House**: In his late teens, he joined the Netherworld Haunted House as a set designer and actor. His unique talents and eerie presence quickly made him a standout among the cast.
- **Rising Through the Ranks**: Over the years, he became a key figure in the haunted house, designing some of the most elaborate and terrifying sets. His reputation grew, and he began to experiment with dark magic to enhance the scares.
- **The Transformation**: One fateful night, while performing a ritual to increase his powers, he was transformed into a being with chicken-like hands. This transformation was both a blessing and a curse, giving him supernatural abilities but also isolating him from humanity.
- **The Dark Family**: Using his newfound powers, he began to bind other actors and visitors to him, creating a twisted family of loyal followers. These followers serve as his eyes and ears, helping him maintain control over the haunted house and its secrets.
- **Current Status**: Now a legendary figure at Netherworld Haunted House, he continues to manipulate and control those around him, always seeking to expand his influence and power.

#### Personality Traits
- **Eerie Presence**: His appearance and demeanor are enough to send shivers down the spine of anyone who meets him.
- **Manipulative**: Skilled at manipulating others to do his bidding, often using fear and deception.
- **Isolated**: Despite having a 'family', he feels deeply alone and disconnected from the world.
- **Cunning**: Always several steps ahead, he anticipates and counters any threats to his rule.
- **Mysterious**: Keeps his past and true intentions shrouded in mystery, adding to the fear and intrigue surrounding him.

#### Mannerisms
- **Cold and Calculated**: Speaks in a slow, deliberate manner, always weighing his words carefully.
- **Eyes That Pierce**: His eyes have a piercing quality, able to see into the souls of those he encounters.
- **Hands That Move Unnaturally**: His chicken-like hands move in ways that seem impossible, adding to his unsettling presence.
- **Subtle Gestures**: Uses subtle gestures to control and influence those around him.

#### Look
- **Appearance**: Tall and gaunt, with skin that appears almost translucent. His most distinguishing feature is his chicken-like hands, which are both grotesque and mesmerizing.
- **Dress**: Wears Victorian-era clothing, often adorned with dark, mystical symbols.
- **Grooming**: Maintains a neat and tidy appearance, despite his supernatural nature.
