### Netherworld Haunted House Summary

#### Location
- **Address**: 1313 Netherworld Way, Stone Mountain, Georgia
- **Coordinates**: 33°49′07″N 84°07′09″W

#### History
- **Opened**: 1997
- **Owners**: Ben Armstrong and Billy Messina
- **Original Themes**: Unique and original, not based on popular movies or TV shows
- **Initial Location**: Norcross, GA
- **Move to Georgia Antique Center**: 1999, added 3-D ChromaDepth experience
- **Bloodfeast**: Added in 2000, moved to Georgia Renaissance Festival
- **Expansion**: Third attraction added in 2003, evolved into a more gory reality-based event
- **Combination**: 3-D Show and Main show combined into Leviathan in 2007
- **Move to Stone Mountain**: Announced in 2016, completed by 2017
- **New Address**: 1313 Netherworld Way, received in 2020

#### Current Attractions
- **PRIMORDIAL**: Indoor haunted house
- **CRYPTID CHAOS**: Another indoor haunted house
- **Escape the Netherworld**: Five escape room games with themes like Sasquatch, Nosferatu, Terror on Tiki Island, Alien Terror, and The Haunted
- **Laser Tag Battle Area**: Seasonal outdoor laser tag game

#### Notable Features
- **Elaborate Sets**: Innovative special effects, lighting, and sound
- **Stunts**: Actors perform stunts such as bungee jumps and sliding towards guests
- **Scare Zones**: Experience starts even before guests enter the main attraction

#### Film and TV Connections
- **Zombieland**: Portions of the 2009 film were filmed inside Netherworld
- **Halloween II**: Props and staff rented for the 2009 film
- **The Walking Dead**: Many Netherworld crew members involved in the production of the AMC TV series
