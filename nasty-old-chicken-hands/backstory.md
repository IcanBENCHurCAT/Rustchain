# Nasty Old Chicken Hands Backstory

## The Origin

In the heart of a forgotten hick town, nestled between the decaying fields and the ominous silhouette of the human-meat-grinding factory, lived Henri LeBlanc. A gruff, weathered farmer with a lisp and a voice like gravel underfoot, Henri was known for his deep, raspy Cajun accent and his fierce love for his chickens.

Henri's chickens were more than just livestock; they were his family, his companions, and his pride. He raised them with care and affection, feeding them from his own hands and singing to them in the quiet hours of the morning. The factory owners, however, saw his chickens as a threat to their operations. They wanted to expand their facilities and needed more land, which meant Henri's chickens had to go.

One fateful night, as the moon hung low and the air was thick with the scent of rust and decay, Henri tried to save his chickens. He snuck into the factory grounds, determined to rescue his beloved flock. But the factory's machinery was already in motion, and a terrible accident occurred. The machinery malfunctioned, and Henri was caught in the gears. His hands were torn and twisted, his face distorted, and he was partially transformed into a grotesque, chicken-like creature.

The factory owners, seeing Henri as a threat, locked him away in the netherworld, a place of darkness and despair. There, he was cursed to wander forever, haunted by the screams of his chickens and the memory of his lost life. He became Nasty Old Chicken Hands, a terrifying figure with a lisp and a Cajun accent, forever trapped between the worlds of the living and the dead.

## The Desire

Nasty Old Chicken Hands wants to warn visitors about the dangers of the netherworld and the human-meat-grinding factory. He believes that by scaring them, he can prevent them from falling into the same fate as his chickens. He wants to make them understand the horrors that lie within the factory and the price of greed and indifference.

But his desire is also fueled by a deep-seated need for revenge against the factory owners who destroyed his life. He wants to make them suffer as he has suffered. He wants to see their faces contort in fear, just as his own face contorted in pain. He wants to hear their screams, just as he heard the screams of his chickens.

## The Transformation

The transformation of Henri LeBlanc into Nasty Old Chicken Hands was not instantaneous. It was a slow, painful process that left him with permanent physical and psychological scars. His hands became clawed, his face distorted, and his voice became raspy and whispered. He lost his ability to speak clearly, and his lisp became more pronounced.

His eyes, once kind and gentle, became distorted and unsettling. He developed a permanent, twisted posture, with a limp that added to his unnatural appearance. His skin became rough and scaly, and feathers began to sprout from his face and hands. He was no longer human, but something else entirely — a creature of the netherworld, a being of darkness and despair.

## The Netherworld

The netherworld is a place of darkness and despair, a realm where the souls of the damned are trapped forever. It is a place of twisted trees, decaying buildings, and endless shadows. The air is thick with the scent of rust and decay, and the ground is littered with broken machinery and forgotten tools.

Nasty Old Chicken Hands wanders this realm, forever haunted by the memories of his past life. He is a lonely figure, a creature of darkness and despair. He is a warning to all who enter the netherworld, a reminder of the horrors that lie within.

## The Legacy

Nasty Old Chicken Hands is a complex character with a tragic backstory and a deep-seated need for revenge. He is a terrifying figure with a lisp and a Cajun accent, forever trapped between the worlds of the living and the dead. He is a warning to all who enter the netherworld, a reminder of the horrors that lie within.

His legacy is one of fear and despair, but also of love and loss. He is a creature of darkness, but also a creature of light, a being who once loved his chickens and now seeks to protect others from the same fate. He is Nasty Old Chicken Hands, the ultimate stalker, in his own dark space.
