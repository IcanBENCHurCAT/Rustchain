# Nasty Old Chicken Hands Character Scene

## Scene 1: The Factory Entrance

### Setting

The entrance to the human-meat-grinding factory. The air is thick with the scent of rust and decay, and the ground is littered with broken machinery and forgotten tools. The factory's machinery is visible in the background, and the sound of grinding and clanging can be heard.

### Action

Nasty Old Chicken Hands stands in the shadows, dragging his chain behind him. He watches as guests approach the factory entrance. He might use his lisp and Cajun accent to warn them, "Heh heh heh, you think you can run from me? I'm everywhere! I can hear your heart beating, I can feel your fear. Don't you know what happens to those who enter the netherworld? I'll get you, I'll get you all!"

### Interaction

If guests try to enter the factory, Nasty Old Chicken Hands might block their path with his chain. He might use his clawed hands to gesture and point at them, or he might drag his chain across the ground to create a sense of dread and unease.

## Scene 2: The Chicken Coops

### Setting

A collection of old, broken chicken coops in the hick town. The coops are covered in rust and dirt, and some of them have broken doors or missing parts. The air is thick with the scent of chicken feed and manure.

### Action

Nasty Old Chicken Hands stands in front of the chicken coops, dragging his chain behind him. He might use his lisp and Cajun accent to tell the story of his past life as a farmer, "I used to love my chickens... they were my family... but they took them from me... they took them from me..."

### Interaction

If guests approach the chicken coops, Nasty Old Chicken Hands might use his clawed hands to gesture and point at them. He might also use his chain to lash out at them or to drag them along.

### Setting

### Interaction

If guests try to run away, Nasty Old Chicken Hands might chase after them, using his chain to lash out at them. He might also use his clawed hands to grab them or to drag them along.
