# Nasty Old Chicken Hands Character Concept

## Character Name
Old Cluck

## Appearance
Old Cluck appears as a hunched, emaciated figure with wrinkled, yellowish skin resembling aged chicken skin. His hands are grotesquely large, with long, claw-like fingers that seem to move independently. His eyes are small, beady, and yellow, giving him a predatory look. He wears tattered, oil-stained overalls with a faded "Poultry Processing" patch. His feet are cloven, resembling chicken feet, and he walks with a stiff, jerky gait.

## Backstory
Old Cluck was once a poultry processing plant worker who became obsessed with the idea of perfecting chicken meat. He experimented with various preservation techniques, using a secret formula that involved ancient alchemical ingredients. In a failed experiment, he transformed himself into a half-human, half-chicken abomination. Trapped in his grotesque form, he now wanders the Netherworld, seeking revenge on those who laughed at his failed experiments and searching for a way to reverse the transformation.

## Motivations
- Revenge on those who mocked his work
- Finding a cure to reverse his transformation
- Protecting his secret formula
- Creating the perfect chicken meat (his obsession)

## Mannerisms
- Speaks in a raspy, guttural voice with occasional squawks
- Moves his hands in a jerky, clawing motion
- Frequently sniffs the air, searching for the scent of chicken
- Has a habit of pecking at the ground when frustrated
- Occasionally mutters about "perfecting the formula"

## Personality
Old Cluck is paranoid, obsessive, and deeply insecure about his appearance. He's intelligent but consumed by his obsession with chicken meat. He's quick to anger when his secret formula is threatened but can be surprisingly gentle when discussing his work. He views humans as both potential threats and subjects for his experiments.

## Role in Netherworld Haunted House
Old Cluck serves as a unique, character-driven horror element in the haunted house. He's not just a monster to be scared of, but a tragic figure with a compelling backstory. Visitors might encounter him in a dimly lit processing room, where he's attempting to perfect his formula, or in a hidden laboratory filled with chicken-themed experiments. His presence adds depth to the haunted house experience, creating a more immersive and memorable encounter than a typical jump-scare monster.
