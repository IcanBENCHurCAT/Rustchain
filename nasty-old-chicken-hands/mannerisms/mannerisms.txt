### Mannerisms and Behaviors for Nasty Old Chicken Hands

1. **Fidgeting with Fingers**: Constantly picks at his scaly, wrinkled fingers, often leaving them bloody.
2. **Eating Habits**: Prefers to eat raw meat, often picking bones clean with his teeth.
3. **Speaking Voice**: Has a raspy, gravelly voice that sounds like old wood being scraped.
4. **Posture**: Hunches over, leaning on a cane or nearby objects for support.
5. **Eye Contact**: Avoids eye contact, looking down or away when speaking to others.
6. **Smell**: Emits a strong, unpleasant odor of decay and old chicken feed.
7. **Walking Gait**: Shuffles along slowly, dragging his feet and making a scraping sound.
8. **Laugh**: Has a cackling, sinister laugh that sends shivers down the spine.
9. **Touch**: His hands feel cold and clammy, causing discomfort when he touches others.
10. **Personal Space**: Invades personal space, getting too close to people when speaking or interacting.
11. **Clothing**: Wears tattered, dirty clothes that have seen better days.
12. **Hygiene**: Poor hygiene, rarely washes his hands or face.
13. **Habits**: Chews on a piece of straw or a feather, spitting it out occasionally.
14. **Gestures**: Makes sweeping gestures with his hands, as if trying to clear the air around him.
15. **Expressions**: Often has a sly, cunning expression, with a perpetual smirk.
