# Nasty Old Chicken Hands Character Arc

## Act 1: The Farmer

### Setting

The hick town outside the human-meat-grinding factory. The air is thick with the scent of chicken feed and manure, and the ground is covered in dirt and straw. Henri LeBlanc, a gruff, weathered farmer with a lisp and a Cajun accent, tends to his chickens.

### Action

Henri loves his chickens. He feeds them from his own hands and sings to them in the quiet hours of the morning. He is a kind and gentle man, but he is also fierce and protective of his flock. He knows that the factory owners want to expand their facilities and need more land, which means his chickens have to go.

### Interaction

Henri tries to save his chickens. He snuck into the factory grounds, determined to rescue his beloved flock. But the factory's machinery was already in motion, and a terrible accident occurred. The machinery malfunctioned, and Henri was caught in the gears.

## Act 2: The Transformation

### Setting

The human-meat-grinding factory. The air is thick with the scent of rust and decay, and the ground is littered with broken machinery and forgotten tools. Henri is caught in the gears, his hands torn and twisted, his face distorted.

### Action

Henri is partially transformed into a grotesque, chicken-like creature. His hands become clawed, his face distorted, and he is cursed to wander the netherworld, forever haunted by the screams of his chickens.

### Interaction

The factory owners, seeing Henri as a threat, lock him away in the netherworld. There, he is cursed to wander forever, haunted by the memories of his past life. He becomes Nasty Old Chicken Hands, a terrifying figure with a lisp and a Cajun accent, forever trapped between the worlds of the living and the dead.

## Act 3: The Haunted House

### Setting

The netherworld, a place of darkness and despair. The air is thick with the scent of rust and decay, and the ground is littered with broken machinery and forgotten tools. Nasty Old Chicken Hands wanders this realm, forever haunted by the memories of his past life.

### Action

Nasty Old Chicken Hands wants to warn visitors about the dangers of the netherworld and the human-meat-grinding factory. He believes that by scaring them, he can prevent them from falling into the same fate as his chickens. He wants to make them understand the horrors that lie within the factory and the price of greed and indifference.

### Interaction

Nasty Old Chicken Hands targets the most frightened guests, usually those in the middle or back of a group. He avoids the front-runners, who often act as "blockers" and are less likely to be scared. He improvises his performance based on the guests' reactions, using his lisp and Cajun accent to unnerve them.

## Act 4: The Final Confrontation

### Setting

The heart of the netherworld, a place of darkness and despair. The air is thick with the scent of rust and decay, and the ground is littered with broken machinery and forgotten tools. The factory's machinery is visible in the background, and the sound of grinding and clanging can be heard.

### Action

Nasty Old Chicken Hands stands in the center of the netherworld, dragging his chain behind him. He tells the story of his transformation, "I was once a farmer... I loved my chickens... but they took them from me... they took them from me... and now I am cursed to wander the netherworld, forever haunted by the screams of my chickens..."

### Interaction

If guests try to leave, Nasty Old Chicken Hands might block their path with his chain. He might use his clawed hands to gesture and point at them, or he might drag his chain across the ground to create a sense of dread and unease.

## Conclusion

Nasty Old Chicken Hands' character arc is a tragic one. He was once a kind and gentle farmer who loved his chickens, but he was transformed into a grotesque, chicken-like creature by the factory's machinery. He is now cursed to wander the netherworld, forever haunted by the memories of his past life. His desire to warn visitors about the dangers of the netherworld and the human-meat-grinding factory is driven by a deep-seated need for revenge against the factory owners who destroyed his life. His lisp, Cajun accent, and unique physicality make him a terrifying and memorable presence in the netherworld haunted house.
